Official Terms of Service & Privacy Policy • Last updated: November 2026

1. Terms of Service

Pipoca & Bip Studio is a private, non-commercial content automation tool operated solely by its creator to upload animated short video drafts directly to the owner's authorized TikTok account inbox via the official TikTok Content Posting API.

This application is not offered as a public software-as-a-service (SaaS) and does not provide access to external or third-party end users.

2. Privacy Policy

Data Collection: Pipoca & Bip Studio does not collect, harvest, store, sell, or share personal information, browsing data, or analytics from third-party users.

Authentication & Tokens: OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok are stored exclusively inside the owner's private, self-hosted local environment and are used solely to authenticate draft video uploads to the owner's own channel.

Data Retention & Revocation: Access may be revoked at any time directly through the TikTok account security settings, immediately invalidating stored tokens.
